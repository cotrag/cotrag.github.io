I am a Ph.D. candidate in political science at The Ohio State University, specializing in quantitative methods and causal inference. Before graduate school, I spent two years as an analyst in the financial economics practice at Charles River Associates in Washington, D.C., analyzing consumer lending data for fair lending litigation. I returned to graduate school to deepen the econometric and causal inference methods I had been developing there. My dissertation draws on data from a variety of sources to show how the American public processes economic information and translates it into political evaluations.

## Skills

**Statistical methods:** Causal inference, econometrics, regression modeling, survey methodology, data validation, data visualization

**Programming:** R (advanced), Python, SQL, Stata

**Tools:** DuckDB, Git, high-performance computing (SLURM), ArcGIS, SPSS, LaTeX

## Experience

**Doctoral Researcher**, Department of Political Science, The Ohio State University (2023–present)
- Built a SQL (DuckDB) and R pipeline that sources and curates 90+ statewide voter-file archives into an analysis-ready panel, processing over 400 million records covering 15.8 million registrants across three states
- Apply quasi-experimental research designs in R to estimate how credit availability affects electoral behavior, testing every result against alternative specifications, bandwidths, and control groups
- Translate model output into figures, written summaries, and conference presentations for audiences with varying technical backgrounds

**Fellow**, Program in Statistics and Methodology (PRISM), The Ohio State University (2026–present)
- Lead methods workshops and advise graduate researchers on data cleaning and modeling problems in their own projects

**Graduate Research Assistant**, Comparative National Elections Project, The Ohio State University (2024–2025)
- Validated and integrated survey data collected by field researchers in more than 30 countries into a longitudinal cross-national dataset cited in over 100 articles and books
- Developed standardized coding protocols and data documentation for a distributed international team, then managed their handoff from Ohio State to Pompeu Fabra University

**Analyst / Associate, Financial Economics Practice**, Charles River Associates (2021–2023)
- Built Stata and Python pipelines to clean and analyze consumer lending data for fair lending and discrimination matters, from tens of thousands of HMDA-reportable mortgage and auto records to 20+ million card-level transaction observations
- Produced result tables, exhibits, and written reports communicating statistical findings to clients, opposing counsel, and federal regulators
- Managed 4–5 simultaneous litigation support projects under deadline, with peer code review and quality-assurance checks on every deliverable
- Co-authored a memo on the effect of COVID-era debt management programs on credit card default rates

**Research Assistant**, UBI Center (2021)
